Latency Transparency

What "sub-millisecond"
honestly measures.

Network physics dictates that light in fiber travels ~200 km per millisecond. No global cloud API on Earth can offer a public internet round-trip (RTT) under 1ms. When vendors claim "sub-millisecond API", engineering teams deserve full clarity on where time is spent.

ENGINEERING ADVISORY

The residential proxy reality:
Why nuance builds trust.

Commercial VPNs and hosting datacenters operate on static Autonomous System numbers with dedicated CIDR blocks. Detecting them is deterministic. Residential proxies, however, operate through real home broadband networks, opt-in P2P bandwidth software, or compromised edge gateways.

The Dual-Use Challenge

A single residential IP address can belong to a legitimate family streaming video at 10:00 AM, and route an automated credential stuffing attempt at 10:05 AM through a background proxy client. Blindly banning residential IPs causes severe false positives that damage real revenue.

Continuous Confidence Scoring

Sentinel outputs a continuous confidence float (0.00 – 1.00) alongside standardized reason_codes. This empowers security teams to tune their own threshold curves instead of relying on a rigid binary flag.

Recommended Practice: Graduated Defense Pattern

For high-confidence commercial VPNs or datacenters, execute hard 403 blocks. For residential tunnel signals (RESIDENTIAL_TUNNEL_PROXY), introduce stepped-up verification: challenge with MFA, trigger an invisible CAPTCHA, or clamp rate velocity. Never discard a paying user on a binary guess.
